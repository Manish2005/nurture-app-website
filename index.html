NurturePlant Care

Care reminders for every plant you own

Nurture tells you when each plant needs watering, feeding, pruning or repotting, so nothing gets forgotten and nothing gets drowned.

Download on the App Store Coming soon to Google Play

What it does

  • Reminders on your schedule. Set how often each task repeats and at what time. Nurture sends one reminder for everything due together, and moves the next date on when you mark it done.
  • Identify a plant from a photo. Not sure what you have? Take a picture and Nurture suggests what it is, with its care guide.
  • A care guide for each species. Light, water, soil and the common problems, written to be read in a minute.
  • Your plants stay on your phone. No account needed. Reminders work without a connection, and a backup file keeps everything safe when you change phones.
