Households, caretakers and gifts

A household is a collection of plants that several people care for together. The person who subscribes is its owner. The owner can invite and remove people, can delete plants and history, and can end the household. Members can add and edit plants and complete care; they cannot delete. A caretaker is a person the owner gives time-limited access, who can mark care done and nothing else, and whose access ends on its date. Plants, notes and photos in a household are stored on our server so everyone in it can see them, and are removed from a person's phone when they leave or are removed.

The owner of a household can give a plant to another person with a one-time gift code, valid for 7 days. Nothing is sent to anyone until they accept. When they do, they receive their own copy of the plant with its care tasks, history and photos, and the plant is removed from the giver's phone and household. Until then it stays where it was, and the giver can take the gift back.
